Questions

Good to know before we start.

Have a question that isn’t covered here? Reach out and tell us what you’re working on.

What happens during the free audit?

We take a look at the public-facing parts of your online presence and share a few practical opportunities. It’s an introductory review, not a promise of specific ranking or sales results.

Do I have to buy a package?

No. The audit is an initial conversation. If you decide to move forward, we’ll agree on the work and pricing first.

Do I keep ownership of my accounts?

Yes. Your business should retain ownership of its Google, social media, website, and advertising accounts. Access can be granted for the work being done.

Do you guarantee more customers or top Google rankings?

No ethical marketer can guarantee a specific ranking or number of customers. We focus on improving your online presence and being clear about the work and results.
